# Limitations and Falsification

BUTTERFLY is an experimental perturbation-propagation framework.

## Limitations

- Amplification depends on state normalization.
- Boundary definitions can be arbitrary.
- Some large downstream effects are fully linear.
- Small perturbations may be numerically unstable in simulation.
- Feedback estimates depend on model structure.
- A large emergence score does not imply importance or danger.

## Falsification criteria

BUTTERFLY's central hypothesis is weakened if:

1. Standard sensitivity analysis predicts outcomes equally well.
2. Propagation depth adds no useful information.
3. Boundary crossing does not improve regime-change prediction.
4. Feedback gain does not distinguish recursive from feed-forward amplification.
5. Emergence scores fail on held-out systems.
6. Results are unstable under small changes in normalization.

## Claim boundary

BUTTERFLY measures perturbation sensitivity under a specified model. It does not establish literal chaos, causation, or real-world catastrophic risk without external evidence.
